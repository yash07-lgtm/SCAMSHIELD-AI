      Cybersecurity Safety Tips

      Verify Links Before Clicking

      Hover over links to check the real URL. Legitimate organisations never use URL shorteners or IP addresses in links.

      Be Sceptical of Urgency

      Scammers manufacture panic. "Act now or your account will be closed" is a classic pressure tactic — slow down and verify.

      Never Share Credentials

      No legitimate bank, government body, or tech company will ever ask for your password or PIN via email or SMS.

      Verify the Sender

      Check the full email address, not just the display name. Scammers spoof familiar names with random domains.
